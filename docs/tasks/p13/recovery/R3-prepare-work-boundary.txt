1. Determine which requirements are intrinsic to safely building the Phase → Planning Map → WorkItem association and which are accidental coupling to a manually opened Software Map tab, synthesis cache or transient browser state.

3. If architecture data is truly mandatory for some operations, document exactly why and provide a guided, bounded prerequisite at the right UI step rather than an opaque failure. Do not disable architecture/project/source guards or silently synthesize a whole codebase.
